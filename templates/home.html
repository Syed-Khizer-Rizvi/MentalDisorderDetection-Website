ABOUT

About Mental Disorder Detection

Mental Disorder Detection is an AI-based mental disorder detection and monitoring platform designed to bridge the gap between traditional psychiatric care and modern technology. By combining facial emotion recognition, IoT wristband biometric data, and validated clinical questionnaires, we provide patients and doctors with a continuous, objective window into mental health — privately and without stigma.

Our mission is simple: detect early, intervene faster, and empower both patients and clinicians with the data they need to heal.

FEATURES

Everything You Need for Mental Wellness

Facial Emotion Recognition

Our custom-trained CNN reads 7 emotions from your camera during a check-in. The video is never stored.

Clinical Questionnaires

PHQ-9, GAD-7 and PSS-10: the standard screening tools for depression, anxiety and stress.

PsyMonitor Wristband

PsyMonitor Band v2 streams heart rate, skin response (GSR) and temperature from its ESP32 sensors.

Wellness Games

Calming games plus breathing, journaling and gratitude exercises, right in your dashboard.

Doctor Consultations

Request a consultation or an emergency appointment, and chat with your assigned doctor.

Treatment Plans

Your doctor writes a personal plan with instructions, recommended activities and a follow-up date.

Live Monitoring

Doctors see live wristband readings and are alerted as soon as a High or Critical risk appears.

Privacy-First Design

Video never stored. Only emotion scores are encrypted and saved securely.
