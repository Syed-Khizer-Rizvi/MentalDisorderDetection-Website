About Mental Disorder Detection

Mental Disorder Detection is an AI-based mental disorder detection and monitoring platform designed to bridge the gap between traditional psychiatric care and modern technology. By combining facial emotion recognition, IoT wristband biometric data, and validated clinical questionnaires, we provide patients and doctors with a continuous, objective window into mental health — privately and without stigma.

Our mission is simple: detect early, intervene faster, and empower both patients and clinicians with the data they need to heal.

IoT Wristband Monitoring
