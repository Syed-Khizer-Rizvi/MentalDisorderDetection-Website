For patients

How do I take an assessment?

Sign in to the Patient Portal and choose New Assessment on your dashboard. It has three short parts:

  1. Questionnaires: PHQ-9 (mood), GAD-7 (anxiety) and PSS-10 (stress). Answer honestly about the last two weeks.
  2. Facial scan (optional): your camera reads facial expressions for about 15 seconds. You can skip it.
  3. Results: your scores, an overall risk score and suggested next steps. You can download them as a PDF.

It takes about 8–10 minutes. Past results stay in History.

How does the wristband connect?

The {{ product }} ({{ price }}, cash on delivery) measures heart rate, skin response (GSR) and skin temperature, and sends readings over Wi-Fi.

  1. Order it from the Wristband page.
  2. When it arrives, press Generate Pairing Code on the same page to get a Device ID and an API key.
  3. Enter both in the band's setup along with your Wi-Fi details. The API key is shown only once, so copy it straight away.

The band then sends a reading about every {{ interval_s }} seconds while it's online, and your dashboard and doctor see them. Lost the key? Use Revoke & Regenerate to get a new one.

How can I contact my doctor?
  • Chat: send your doctor a private message any time from the Chat page.
  • Consultations: request a consultation, or an emergency appointment if you need to be seen urgently. Your doctor replies there and in chat.
  • Treatment plan: your doctor's instructions and daily activities appear on your dashboard.

In a crisis, don't wait for a reply: call Umang 0317-4288665 or Rozan 051-2890505.

Is my data private?
  • Only your assigned doctor sees your assessments, and your journal entries only if you choose to share them.
  • The facial scan never stores video, only emotion percentages.
  • Administrators can manage accounts, but chat messages, notes, assessment answers and consultation details are hidden from them.
  • Sign-in is protected, and every sign-in and change is recorded in an audit log.
Can I delete my account?

Yes. Email {{ support_email }} from the address you signed up with and ask for your account to be deleted. An administrator then deletes your sign-in account together with your assessments, chats, clinical notes, alerts and wristband data.

How often should I check in?

The questionnaires ask about the last two weeks, so an assessment every two weeks works well, unless your doctor asks for more often. Day to day, follow the activities in your treatment plan and write a short journal entry. Small, regular check-ins show your progress best.

For doctors

I registered as a doctor. When can I sign in?

New doctor accounts are checked by an administrator first (including your PMC license number). You can sign in to the Doctor Portal as soon as your account is approved. Patients are then assigned to you.

How do risk alerts and live monitoring work?

When one of your patients completes an assessment with a critical score, you get an alert in Risk Alerts and a badge in the sidebar. Monitor Patient shows live wristband vitals for patients who have paired a band, and each patient's page shows their history, notes and treatment plan.
